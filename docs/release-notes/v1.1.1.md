**Changed:**
- Accounts with no 5-hour limit, such as verified Ultra accounts: when Claude Code's /usage reports no 5-hour window, the bar shows 7d and then the most pressing weekly limit of a single model, such as Fable, by its own name. 5h is hidden, and it comes back once a reading includes it. The ? panel counts sessions' shares from the start of the week, lists the reported limits most pressing first, and notes "Claude Code's usage report shows no 5-hour window right now. Verified Ultra accounts don't have one."
- The ? panel's calm line counts the windows it has: "The week is on pace.", "Both windows…" or "All windows…". An extra limit with a pace says its state as 5h and 7d do, such as "Fable week · Learning · 0% used".
- A stop the app queued reports its own outcome: "Stop sent to" a session when the stop command succeeds, and "the app keeps trying" only when it will retry.

**Fixed:**
- Settings › General › "When the app quits" shows its three buttons again. In 1.1.0 it was squeezed to a thin line and couldn't be used.
- A session the app refuses is stopped even when the app can't save to its data folder, as when the disk is full. The app says it couldn't save the refusal, which may then not outlast a restart of the app.
- A new session, a copy or a move can't be started at the whole disk under another name: `/System/Volumes/Data` in any letter case, `/Volumes/Macintosh HD`, a link to either, or a path under `/.vol`, `/.nofollow` or `/.resolve`. The app says "Choose a folder other than the whole disk." An external drive's root, a disk image or a network share is allowed.
- A folder a session adds to its saved settings is refused, not offered for confirmation, when it is `/System`, `/System/Volumes`, a path under `/.vol`, `/.nofollow` or `/.resolve`, a protected folder written through `/System/Volumes/Data`, a path written with `.` or `..`, or a folder whose real path can't be read.
- The Arbiter's daily spending cap holds when several calls run at once: each call holds the most it could cost until its real cost is logged, and a call that could pass the cap isn't made.
- The app's command log keeps a week of session stops, resumes and removals. The usage reading every few minutes is logged only when its outcome changes, with a daily count of the rest.
- Quit from the app's menu saves the command log's last entries before the app closes.

**Known limits:**
- The app checks a session's saved settings, which Claude Code keeps in the session's `state.json`, when it sees a new process for the session. A program running as you could change those settings, restart the session from the command line, and put the settings back before the app's next check a few seconds later. The app doesn't catch that: the process runs on the changed settings, and the app finds the original ones. A later release will watch the saved settings for changes.
