**New:**
- A session switcher at the top of Focus: chips for the last five sessions you opened, plus any you pin. ⌘1–⌘9 open a chip from anywhere, ⌘⇧[ and ⌘⇧] step through them, and ⌘⇧P pins or unpins the session in Focus. Pin from a chip or from the session menu. A pinned session doesn't roll off.
- Sidebar categories stand out: a tinted header and a line in the category's color down its sessions.
- Sessions set to never roll off show ∞ in the sidebar, as on their cards.
- The sidebar can be resized: drag its edge, or double-click the edge to restore it. Names still cut short show in full on hover.
- When sessions aren't connected to the app, a band names them, says what's off, gives the likely cause when the app saw one, and offers Check again and a restart for each.
- A Needs-you card for a session waiting on your reply says, in one line, what it's asking. The Arbiter writes it, only in categories you've allowed to share text with it, and it counts toward the Arbiter's daily cap.
- A pace dot on the 5h and 7d meters shows where your recent rate is heading. When it isn't on pace, "Tight", "Over" or "Out" replaces the countdown. An old reading is marked with its age instead of hidden.
- The ? next to the meters (⌘⇧U) shows what's using your limits: this app's sessions by share, with tips from the Arbiter when it's on.
- A spend figure for the month: simulated from Claude Code's own estimates, or real from your Console organization with a Console key, tested before it's saved (Settings › Spend).
- Settings › About has "Get beta updates".
- The window opens at the size and place you left it, maximized or full screen too, and back on the main display if its old display is gone.

**Changed:**
- Category jumps moved from ⌘1–⌘9 to ⌥⌘1–⌥⌘9. ⌘1–⌘9 now open the switcher's chips.
- "Show empty categories" and "Hide empty categories" replace the empty-categories arrow.
- Clicking a category's name in the sidebar opens Fleet filtered to it, from Focus too; ⌥⌘1–⌥⌘9 do the same.
- A session that hasn't connected to the app loses answering from cards and sending prompts on its own; the other sessions keep both.
- The security and PATH alerts name each session, with an action for each one.
- The Arbiter refuses an Admin API key, and one saved earlier is removed when the app starts. Save a workspace-scoped key in Settings › Arbiter.

**Fixed:**
- A card no longer stays on "Needs you · Question" after the question was answered in the session.
- A session just launched reads "Starting", not "outside the app" and idle, until its first report.
- Launching right after the app starts no longer runs before the login PATH is read.
- A session started by an earlier version of the app can be resumed again, keeping its id and Never roll off, once the app has checked the reporter mod copy it uses.
- The 5h and 7d meters no longer jump between sessions' old readings or go blank. They show the newest reading from the app's own sessions and keep it until a newer one arrives.
- The simulated spend no longer books a session's whole earlier cost to the day the app first saw it. A session started this month counts from its start day; an older one counts from when the app began counting.
